# Proposal: Ultra-Modern Screen Overhaul & 100% Indian Domestic Curation

## Context & Objectives
Based on the 7-page "SPORTS CRICKET APP BRIEF (CRICIT)", the video shorts reel system is verified and stable and must be left intact. However, all non-video screens (`My Feed`, `Domestic`, `International`, `Nxtspot`, `Search`, `Profile`, in-app web sheet, and sponsored ad slots) require a complete visual, architectural, and content overhaul.

The goal is to deliver an ultra-modern, high-polish Inshorts-grade mobile experience specifically tailored for Indian cricket enthusiasts and grassroots scouts.

## Key Requirements
1. **Video Screens Preserved**:
   - `VideoShortView` and associated reel mechanics (gesture isolation, auto-play, auto-advance, infinite card deck swipe, edge-to-edge reel) are kept untouched.
2. **100% Indian Domestic & Grassroots Curation**:
   - `Domestic` tab must feature **strictly 100% Indian domestic tournaments** (Ranji Trophy, Duleep Trophy, Vijay Hazare Trophy, Syed Mushtaq Ali T20, Irani Cup, state leagues like TNPL/Maharaja T20, and WPL/IPL domestic auctions).
   - Zero foreign domestic games (no County, Sheffield Shield, BBL, The Hundred).
   - `Nxtspot` tab must focus strictly on **Indian grassroots cricket** (district championships, academy trials, school leagues like Harris Shield, talent discovery).
3. **60-Word Curated Short Summaries**:
   - Every news card must feature a curated ~60-word summary with punchy headline hooks, media house attribution, author credit, timestamp, and one-tap in-app web reader modal.
4. **Ultra-Modern Visual Design**:
   - Modern typography hierarchy, semantic dark/light color tokens, tactile haptics, elegant category badges, bookmarking, smooth vertical card deck snapping, and "New Feed Available" floating pill.
5. **Enhanced Search & Discovery**:
   - Modern search bar, quick topic chips, breaking headlines list with thumbnails, and talent spotlight.
6. **Feature-Rich Profile & Settings**:
   - User avatar header with fan stats, bookmark collection tabs (All, Articles, Videos), Edit Profile modal, and granular settings (Night Mode, Language, Autoplay, HD Images, Text Size, Notifications).
7. **Ad & Sponsored Placements**:
   - Full-page ad card, bottom footer ad banner, and short post sponsored card with "Tap to know more" CTA.
