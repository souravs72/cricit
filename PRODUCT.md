# PRODUCT.md

## What it is

CRICIT is a mobile app that gives Indian cricket fans every important cricket story as a one-minute
card and a feed of short cricket videos, with a section (Nxtspot) that covers grassroots cricket nobody
else reports on.

## Who it is for

- **Readers:** Indian cricket fans, 16–45, on mid-range Android phones, often on patchy mobile data,
  reading in English or Hindi in short bursts (commute, match breaks).
- **Editors:** a small team (1–5) at the client who review AI cards, publish Nxtspot stories, run ads
  and send push alerts from the CRICIT Newsroom dashboard.

## Jobs

1. "Tell me what happened in cricket in the last few hours, in a minute." (Feed)
2. "Show me the moment everyone is talking about." (Video)
3. "Let my club's match be seen." (Nxtspot)
4. "Get stories out fast without publishing something wrong." (Newsroom)

## What success looks like (first 90 days)

- Day-7 retention above 25%; median session at least 8 cards.
- 95% of cards published within 20 minutes of the source article.
- Zero factual corrections caused by AI-invented numbers.

## Constraints

- Brief: Inshorts-like function and feel, but not a copy; 1–2 unique features (Hindi toggle, Nxtspot).
- Budget ₹90k for the MVP; one VPS; Android first.
- Only AI summaries + credit + link are shown; full articles and videos are never re-hosted.

## Out of scope for v1

iOS release, X/Twitter video, live scores, personalised ranking, comments.
